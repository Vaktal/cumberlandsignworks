# A hat with your logo on it lands differently than anything else you hand out.

Hats get worn. Hats travel. Your logo ends up at the hardware store, the softball field, the
lake — places your business card will never go. And embroidery done right lasts through
dozens of washes without cracking, peeling, or fading out. We've been running the in-house
embroidery machine here in Crossville for 23 years. No outsourcing to a vendor you've never
met. Every stitch goes through our shop.

Small runs are not a problem here. Neither is bringing your own gear.

## How it works

1. **Tell us what you want on it.** Logo, text, or both. Let us know the garment — hat, jacket,
   bag, or something else entirely. We'll digitize your artwork at no extra charge.
2. **We set it up and send a proof.** You see the stitch count, the thread colors, and the
   placement before we run a single hat.
3. **You get it back looking sharp.** Ready for pickup in Crossville or shipped to you.
