# When your whole fleet looks like one company, people start to notice you everywhere.

One wrapped truck is a marketing asset. Ten wrapped trucks that all look like they belong together is a brand statement. It signals that you're an established operation, that your team is organized, and that you take your business seriously. Every time one of your vehicles shows up at a job site, a driveway, a commercial property, or a parking lot across the Upper Cumberland — your name is there, your colors are there, and it all looks intentional.

We've been handling fleet branding for local businesses in Crossville for 23 years. We understand that you can't take a whole fleet offline at once. We understand that vehicles get added, replaced, and retired. And we understand that the goal isn't just to cover everything in vinyl — the goal is to build a look that's consistent today, scalable tomorrow, and durable enough to still look right three years from now.

## Sound familiar?

- Half your trucks have your old logo and half have the new one and none of them match each other.
- You've grown from two vehicles to eight and each one was done by a different shop over the years. They don't look like they belong to the same company.
- You added a new crew and bought three more vans — you need them wrapped fast but you can't pull them all off the road at the same time.
- You want to lock in a consistent design so that every vehicle you add in the future goes on the road looking right, without starting from scratch each time.

Inconsistent fleet branding doesn't just look messy. It makes your company look smaller and less established than it is. That matters when you're bidding against other contractors or trying to build name recognition in a new service area.

## How it works

1. **We start with an audit.** Tell us how many vehicles you have, what types, what colors, and where you stand on branding. We'll assess what you're working with.
2. **We build a master design template.** One coordinated system — your logo, your colors, your contact information, laid out so it translates cleanly across different vehicle types: pickup trucks, cargo vans, box trucks, trailers, whatever's in the fleet.
3. **We phase the installation to keep you moving.** You don't have to bring everything in at once. We work around your schedule — a few vehicles at a time, weekends, or off-season windows — so your crews stay in the field while we work.
4. **You get a design template for future additions.** When you buy a new truck next year, we already have the system built. It goes on fast, consistent, and matched to everything else.
