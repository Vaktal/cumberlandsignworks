# You don't need to cover every inch to make your vehicle work for your business.

A full wrap is a serious investment, and it's the right call for a lot of businesses. But if your truck is already a solid color — white, black, gray — and you need it to say who you are and how to reach you, a well-designed partial wrap or lettering package does the job for less. We've been doing vehicle lettering and partial wraps in Crossville for 23 years. We know how to make four door panels and a tailgate communicate your brand clearly enough that somebody writes down your number at a stoplight.

The goal isn't coverage. The goal is recognition. A person following your truck on 127 toward Crossville should be able to tell who you are, what you do, and how to call you before the light changes.

## Sound familiar?

- You just bought a work truck and you know it needs your name and number on it — you're just not sure what to put where.
- You've got a company van but it only has a small logo stuck to the door. It looks like an afterthought.
- A full wrap is outside the budget right now, but you need something that looks intentional, not amateur.
- You have a few vehicles in different colors and need something that looks consistent without repainting them.

The difference between a vehicle that generates calls and one that doesn't isn't always the budget. It's usually the design and the execution.

## How it works

1. **Tell us about the vehicle and your business.** Year, make, model, color, what your business does, how you want people to contact you. A photo of the truck helps.
2. **We design a layout that works for the space.** We don't just center your logo and call it done. We think about reading distance, vehicle lines, and what information actually drives a call.
3. **We install it in Crossville.** Cut vinyl, printed panels, or a combination — installed clean, sealed at the edges, built to last.

## What we make

- **Door and panel lettering** — business name, phone number, website, and service description in cut vinyl or printed panels
- **Hood graphics** — bold graphic elements that make the vehicle visible in a parking lot or job site
- **Tailgate wraps** — full-color printed tailgate graphic, one of the most-seen surfaces on any pickup truck
- **Partial side panels** — printed graphic covering a portion of the side panels, color-matched or contrasting
- **Cab and roof graphics** — for commercial vans, service trucks, and box trucks where the top panels get seen from above
- **Single-color vinyl lettering** — clean, readable, cost-effective for a first vehicle or a tight budget
- **Magnetic signs** — we make them, though we'll also tell you when it's worth upgrading to something permanent
